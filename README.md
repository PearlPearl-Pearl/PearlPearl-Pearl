# Hi there, I'm Pearl 👋🏾

Mathematician → Data Scientist → ML Researcher 🚀

My work sits at the intersection of mathematics, machine learning, and combinatorial optimization. 
I am interested in understanding how learning-based methods can solve structured problems, 
especially problems involving graphs and discrete optimization.

I completed my MSc at AIMS Rwanda, where my thesis explores graph neural networks 
for solving NP-hard combinatorial optimization problems. My research focuses on combining ideas 
from graph theory, optimization, and machine learning to build models that can reason about complex structures.

Currently exploring PhD opportunities in graph learning, geometric machine learning, and 
learning-augmented optimization.

---

## 🔭 What I'm working on

- 📝 **Master's Thesis**  
  Reproducing and extending GCON, a hybrid-filter graph neural network framework for 
  combinatorial optimization problems including Max Cut, Max Clique, and Minimum Dominating Set.

- 🔍 **Research questions**  
  Investigating how graph structure, architectural choices, and problem-specific inductive biases 
  affect the ability of GNNs to solve combinatorial optimization problems.

- 📚 **Research notes**  
  Writing about mathematics, graph learning, optimization, and ideas I encounter while exploring 
  the field.
